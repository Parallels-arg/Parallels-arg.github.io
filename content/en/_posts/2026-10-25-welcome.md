Welcome! This is where I publish **writeups** for CTFs and machines, reverse engineering and cybersecurity notes.

## What you'll find here

- **Writeups**: HackTheBox and TryHackMe machines and CTF challenges, step by step.
- **Reversing**: crackmes and binary analysis.
- **Notes**: techniques and cheatsheets I use often.

Browse by [categories]({{ "/categories/" | relative_url }}) or the [archive]({{ "/archives/" | relative_url }}), or use the search bar. There is more about me on the [About]({{ "/about/" | relative_url }}) page.

> Writeups for active machines or challenges are published **only after** they are retired, following each platform's rules.
